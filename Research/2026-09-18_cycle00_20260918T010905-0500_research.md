## Methods to consider

### 1. Plane-aware multi-series aggregation

**Why it should help:** Sagittal/coronal/axial emphasize different structures (ACL sagittal, MCL coronal, PF OA axial). Pooling all series together dilutes plane-specific signal and hurts macro AUC equally across 12 targets.

**How to apply:** Encode slices → series emb → fixed plane slots + availability mask; never treat missing plane as clinical negative.

### 2. Weak supervision from multilingual reports

**Why it should help:** Only ~58 gold labels vs thousands of reports. Ignoring reports leaves ranking models undertrained; hard pseudo-labels from negation mistakes invert targets.

**How to apply:** Negation/uncertainty/temporality-aware parser → soft labels + confidence weights; calibrate on gold folds only; never use test reports.

### 3. Small-n gold-set metadata logistic

**Why it should help:** Hand-tuned plane/fluid offsets only moved public macro AUC 0.494→0.499. The 58 gold labels fitted a 7-d ridge logistic that reached 0.514. That is a fallback, not the day's first cycle.

**How to apply:** Keep gold_meta_logit as fallback. Do not resubmit it as cycle 0; ablate ranking changes on top of gold_rank_interact (0.517).

### 4. Plane × protocol interaction ranks

**Why it should help:** Additive sag/cor/ax fractions plus a global fluid fraction cannot represent 'sagittal fluid-sensitive' (ACL/meniscus/effusion) vs 'axial fluid-sensitive' (PF OA, Baker's). gold_rank_interact (0.60·7-d + 0.40·interact) is the frozen public floor at 0.517.

**How to apply:** Keep the 13-d sag/cor/ax × fluid and × fat ridge (λ=3.5). Next ablation is blend weight, not a new architecture.

### 5. Rank-blend weight ablation (0.50/0.50)

**Why it should help:** Raising interact weight from 0.00 (0.514) to 0.40 (0.517) helped. Equal 0.50/0.50 is the smallest untested ranking change that still keeps the frozen 7-d head. 0.70/0.30 is the backup if interact is noisy on rare labels.

**How to apply:** Reuse both fitted heads; rank-transform; blend w·rank(7-d)+(1-w)·rank(interact); map through prevalence; no Gaussian noise.

### 6. Anatomy-oriented multi-task MRI (KAMRNet / slice transformers)

**Why it should help:** Xie et al. 2025 (eClinicalMedicine) trained KAMRNet on 13,419 patients / 14,962 exams for nine knee abnormalities and reported internal primary AUCs ~0.90; a 2026 23-condition slice-transformer (European Radiology) reached median external AUC ~0.78. Those numbers are the visual-model ceiling, not a metadata ceiling. We cannot spend quota on them until real train DICOMs/JPEGs + GPU time are mounted.

**How to apply:** When pixels are available: plane-slot encoder, anatomy-oriented localization, then rank-ensemble with gold_rank_interact rather than replacing it.

### 7. Per-target calibrated prevalence prior blend

**Why it should help:** Macro AUC cares about ranking per target. Pure constant prevalence (~0.49 public) is a floor; blending study-level model logits with target prevalence stabilizes rare labels (MCL, Baker's).

**How to apply:** p = clip((1-α)·σ(logit) + α·prev_t); tune α on OOF; larger α for rare/unstable heads.

### 8. Fluid-sensitive / fat-suppression series routing

**Why it should help:** train_series.csv exposes Fluid_Sensitive and Fat_Suppression. Contusion/effusion/synovitis benefit from fluid-sensitive series; bone findings may prefer others.

**How to apply:** Weight series embeddings by sequence flags before plane pooling; ablate via ASRA H-routing.

### 9. Frozen encoder then last-block unfreeze

**Why it should help:** Tiny gold set overfits if the full backbone trains early. Frozen ImageNet/RadImageNet encoder + heads first is the reliable baseline.

**How to apply:** Train heads only until OOF beats prevalence; then unfreeze last block at low LR.

### 10. Target-specific view preference soft regularizer

**Why it should help:** Hard rules (no sagittal ⇒ ACL=0) destroy recall. Soft preferred-plane coverage features improve ranking without forcing zeros.

**How to apply:** Concatenate preferred-plane coverage; small consistency loss only after ablation accepts it.

### 11. Ensemble fold checkpoints + rank average

**Why it should help:** Single fold on 58 labels is noisy. Probability or rank ensembling across folds lifts macro AUC more than architecture churn.

**How to apply:** Save fold*.pt; at inference mean probs or average ranks then rescale to [eps,1-eps].
